Privacy Policy

MarginMart by K.D. Foods · Wholesale distributor, Bhilai, Chhattisgarh, India · Last updated 12 November 2026

Who we are

K.D. Foods ("MarginMart", "we") is a wholesale distributor of drinks, water, milk drinks and more in Bhilai. This policy covers our website and app (kdfoods.vercel.app), our Facebook Page "MarginMart", our Instagram account @marginmartofficial and the tools we use to post content and reply to messages.

What we collect

  • Shop customers: shop name, owner name, phone number, address/area, orders, bills and payments, so we can deliver and keep accounts.
  • Messages and comments: when you message or comment on our Facebook Page or Instagram account, we receive your public name/username and the message, so we can reply (for example to send our rate list).
  • Staff logins: name, phone and role of our own staff.

How we use it

  • To take orders, deliver, bill and keep the khata (credit ledger).
  • To reply to your messages and comments, and to send you rates and offers you asked for.
  • To show our ads to our existing shop customers on Facebook/Instagram. For this, phone numbers are converted to an irreversible code (SHA-256 hash) before they are sent to Meta; Meta uses the code only to match and then deletes it.

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the services that run our business: Supabase (database), Vercel (website), GitHub (automation), Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Telegram (our internal team chat) and Google Gemini (writing captions; no customer data is sent).

How long we keep it

Business records (bills, payments) are kept as long as the law requires for accounts. Message data is kept only as long as needed to reply and follow up.
