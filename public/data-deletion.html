How to delete your data

To delete the data MarginMart (K.D. Foods) holds about you, including messages or comments you sent to our Facebook Page or Instagram account:

  1. Send a message to 96300 12636 (call or WhatsApp), or message our Facebook Page / Instagram @marginmartofficial, saying "Delete my data".
  2. Tell us your name and the phone number or Facebook/Instagram name you used.
  3. We delete it within 30 days and confirm to you. Bills that the law requires us to keep for accounts are kept only for that purpose.

You can also remove our access from your Facebook settings: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.
