## What the difference is worth

- **On our 3 Nov quantities**, R G's prices would have cost **₹110 more** in total for the common products: we save ₹1,538 on Nimbooz, cans, 750 ml, 1.25 L, Tropicana Slice and CB Milk, but pay ₹1,428 more on 400 ml PET and Sting 250 ml.
- **On R G's quantities**, our prices would cost **₹258 more**, because R G buys a lot of Sting 250 ml (600 cases), where we pay ₹4 more.

## Why the averages differ

- R G's ₹35 average is driven by **600 cases of Sting 250 ml** (₹36) and **Sting 180 ml cans** (₹140).
- Our ₹33.7 includes **240 cases of Aquafina 1 L** (₹30) and **75 of Aquafina 500 ml** (₹25), which R G didn't buy.
- Product for product, we are **cheaper on 6 of 8 common items** and dearer only on 400 ml PET and Sting 250 ml.
