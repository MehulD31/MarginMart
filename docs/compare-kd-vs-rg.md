## What the difference is worth

- **On our quantities**, R G's prices would have cost **₹334 less** in total for the common products: we save ₹2,238 (Pepsi 400 ml, Nimbooz, cans, 750 ml, 1.25 L, Tropicana Slice, CB Milk) but pay ₹2,572 more on other 400 ml flavours and Sting 250 ml (486 cases × ₹4 = ₹1,944).
- **On R G's quantities**, our prices would cost **₹117 less** than R G paid.
- In short, the two price lists come out **almost equal**; the gap depends on how much Sting 250 ml is bought.

## Why the averages differ

- R G's ₹35 average is driven by **600 cases of Sting 250 ml** (₹36) and **Sting 180 ml cans** (₹140).
- Our ₹34.1 includes **200 cases of Aquafina 1 L** (₹30) and **75 of Aquafina 500 ml** (₹25), which R G didn't buy.
- Product for product, we are **cheaper on 7 of 9 common items** and dearer only on other 400 ml flavours and Sting 250 ml.
