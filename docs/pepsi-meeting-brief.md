## 5. What we will ask for

1. **₹11 support in writing**, on **every product**.

5. **Launch support:** free stock, display material, coolers for new shops.

**Our bottom line:** if the support is less than ₹____ per case, or comes with conditions we can't meet, the plan needs to be rethought.
